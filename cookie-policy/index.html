    What are cookies?

    Cookies are small files stored by your browser when you visit a website, including places linked to Winorio reviews. They can save an identifier, a language choice, or a record that helps the site recognise a returning device. Some disappear when you close the browser, while others stay for a set period. They do not read your personal files or change your device on their own. Instead, they let the website remember parts of a visit so pages behave consistently.

    How and why cookies are used

    Cookies on Casino Winorio are used for a variety of functional reasons. Preference cookies allow us to remember settings such as language or display so you do not have to re-select them every time. On occasion, we may use cookies that help measure whether a message was seen (marketing cookies). You may control those through your browser settings or controls on the site.
